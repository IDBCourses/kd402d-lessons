Introduction · This morning, in one picture

The melody, the sound, the steps

an array · many of a kind, by position
an object · one thing, by label
an array of objects · position, then label

Click an expression. What comes back: one note, a whole object, or nothing?

 

Square brackets and a number: a position. A dot and a name: a label.

Recap the morning in five minutes, through the instrument. The melody was the array: many notes, reached by where they sit. One sound was the object: one thing, reached by what each part is called. And the steps put them together: position first, then label. Click through the expressions with the class and ask for a prediction first. The last two are the ones to linger on: steps[1] gives back the whole step, an object; steps.note is undefined because steps is the list, and a list has positions, not labels. That's the most common mistake of the afternoon.
